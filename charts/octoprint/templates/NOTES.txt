Webcam Stream (go2rtc - MJPEG):
  go2rtc is running on port 1984 (API, MJPEG, WebRTC signaling, HLS, snapshots).

  To configure OctoPrint's webcam (Settings -> Plugins -> Classic Webcam), use:
    Stream URL:   http://<node-ip>:1984/api/stream.mjpeg?src=stream  (stream type: MJPEG, recommended)
    Snapshot URL: http://127.0.0.1:1984/api/frame.jpeg?src=stream
    WebRTC URL:   webrtc://<node-ip>:1984/api/webrtc?src=stream  (stream type: WebRTC - lowest latency)
    HLS URL:      http://<node-ip>:1984/api/stream.m3u8?src=stream  (stream type: HLS)

  NOTE: Stream URLs must be reachable from your browser - use your node's IP or
  hostname. The Snapshot URL is fetched server-side by OctoPrint so 127.0.0.1
  works there.

  NOTE: MJPEG is recommended for OctoPrint over HLS/WebRTC as go2rtc's HLS
  sessions expire quickly and WebRTC requires ICE negotiation. The camera sidecar
  captures MJPEG natively (-c:v copy passthrough) so no re-encoding occurs.

  To view the stream from outside the cluster (e.g. your laptop):
    export STREAM_POD=$(kubectl get pods --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l "app.kubernetes.io/component=mjpg-streamer" -o jsonpath="{.items[0].metadata.name}")
    kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward $STREAM_POD 1984:1984

    Then open http://127.0.0.1:1984/api/stream.mjpeg?src=stream (MJPEG)
    or http://127.0.0.1:1984 (go2rtc web UI)
{{- end }}
